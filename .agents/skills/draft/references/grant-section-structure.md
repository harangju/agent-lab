# Grant Section Structure

Funder-agnostic spine for proposals. Map these moves onto the CFP's required headings; do not invent parallel sections that fight the portal form. For sentence rhythm and signpost phrases, also follow the project's `grants/style.md` when present.

## Core Arc

1. Problem.
   - Concrete setting and who feels the pain.
   - Cost of the status quo in plain terms.

2. Gap.
   - What current theory, tools, or evidence cannot do.
   - Why the gap blocks progress now.

3. Ask.
   - The proposal's governing question or aim in one sentence.
   - Prefer "This proposal asks..." / "In this project, we..." over mission fog.

4. Approach.
   - How you will answer the ask: studies, instruments, data, theory.
   - Walk the reader through the sequence; each study block should have a job.

5. Why this team / why now.
   - Distinctive capability, data-in-hand, or timing.
   - Evidence of feasibility beats biography.

6. Impact and falsifiability.
   - What changes if you succeed (science, practice, education as required).
   - How the program could fail cleanly or refine the theory; frame uncertainty as tests, not apology.

## Study / Aim Block Recipe

For each study, aim, or thrust:

1. Job of this block in the larger ask.
2. Design or method in one short pass.
3. What you will measure or produce.
4. What decision or claim it enables.
5. Link to the next block.

## Practical Compression

When page limits are brutal:

1. Problem + gap (merged).
2. Ask.
3. Approach as numbered studies (one tight paragraph each).
4. Impact in one closing paragraph.

Cut education/broader-impacts boilerplate until the CFP demands it; then give one concrete plan, not a catalog.

## Editing Checks

- Every paragraph should have a visible job (problem, cost, measurement, comparison, program).
- Empty transitions ("This matters," "The timing is right") lose; name the reason.
- Do not outrun preliminary data: promise what the design can deliver.
- Related work should create contrast, not a literature review for its own sake.
- Align aims, methods, and budget narrative; orphan aims are a reject signal.
