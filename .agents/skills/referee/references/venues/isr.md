# Information Systems Research (ISR)

The form is on ScholarOne (`mc.manuscriptcentral.com/isr`). Manuscript IDs look like `ISRE-2026-4528`.

## Form fields, in order

1. **Review Summary**: nine questions, each scored 1–5 (1 = Strongly disagree … 5 = Strongly agree) or N/A:
   - Is the topic appropriate for Information Systems Research?
   - Is the paper intellectually stimulating?
   - Is the literature review appropriate?
   - Is the design (procedure, methods) adequate and appropriate?
   - Is the analysis of data appropriate?
   - Are the conclusions reasonable?
   - Is there a theoretical contribution?
   - Is the presentation clear and readable?
   - Does the article have applied value?
2. **Recommendation**: a dropdown. "Reject" is one option. The others haven't been seen, so don't list or guess them.
3. **Confidential Comments to the Editor** (singular "Editor"). The authors never see it.
4. **Comments to the Author**: one text box. The Summary, Major Comments, and Minor Comments all go here.
5. **Files attached**: an optional upload.
6. **Web of Science recognition**: yes or no. It's the user's choice, so don't fill it in.

## Notes

- The scores and the recommendation belong to the user. Leave the score column blank unless their notes give the numbers.
- In a second round, the form also shows an **Author's Response** with the authors' files attached. Read it before drafting.

## Review file

Use this only when the repo has no template or earlier review for this venue. If it has either, follow that instead.

Above the body, add `## Review Summary`: a table of the nine questions above with an empty Score column. Body: the generic review template in formats.md, ending with `## Confidential Comments to the Editor`.
