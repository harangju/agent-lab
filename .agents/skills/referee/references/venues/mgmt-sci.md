# Management Science

The form is on ScholarOne (`mc.manuscriptcentral.com/ms`). Manuscript IDs look like `MS-INS-2026-04314`. The middle part names the department: INS (Information Systems), MKG, OPM, and so on. In the user's repo, the folder is `mnsc/`.

## Form fields, in order

1. **Six question prompts**, each with its own text box. Quote them exactly:
   - Please provide a short summary of what you see as the key argument in the paper.
   - Research question and contribution: Is the question clear? Is it important (why/why not)? What contribution does answering the question represent? Is it significant or incremental?
   - Method: Is the method used well justified for the question? Is the method applied convincingly (if not, why not)? Does it produce convincing/robust results that answer the question?
   - Potential impact: Do the results have relevant managerial or policy implications that are potentially important (not necessarily directly, but possibly through a translation), or does the method itself represent a significant innovation? What are the implications? Are they novel and/or significant?
   - Writing: Is the paper written at a level of clarity, articulation, logic, and exposition that is appropriate for a major journal?
   - Do you have any other important comments about the paper?
2. **Recommendation**: the options haven't been seen, so don't list or guess them.
3. **Confidential Comments to the Associate Editor**. The authors never see it.
4. **Comments to the Author**: one text box. The Summary, Major Comments, and Minor Comments all go here.
5. **Files attached**, **Web of Science recognition** (yes or no, the user's choice), and in a second round the **Author's Response**. These work the same as on ISR's form.

## Notes

- Keep the answers to the prompts short: two to four sentences each. They state the verdict, such as "the design is clean, but the measures of both objectives need work". The detailed comments below make the case. Don't repeat the majors in the prompt answers.
- The user usually leaves "any other important comments" blank.

## Review file

Use this only when the repo has no template or earlier review for this venue. If it has either, follow that instead.

Above the body, add the six prompts as bold bullets (`* **<prompt>**`), each with its answer on the next line. Body: the generic review template in formats.md. Rename the last heading `## Confidential Comments to the Associate Editor`.
