# Figure Titles & Captions Craft Guide (Universal Core)

Follow `../../draft/references/STYLE.md`, target-venue exemplars, and the matching playbook in `../../draft/references/genres/` whenever writing or auditing figure captions and panel titles.

## Caption Skeleton

1. **Bold declarative takeaway title** (1 sentence, plain English, no hype): state *what the figure shows* or *what mechanism/effect it establishes*, not just a generic topic label.
2. **Panel-by-panel walkthrough (`A` / `B` / `C`):** One concise sentence per panel stating the manipulation or comparison shown and defining any visual cue or shorthand used in the graphic.
3. **Method & uncertainty note (1 sentence):** State sample size (`N`), error-bar or interval definition, and key scope cleanly at the end—avoid scattering repeated parentheticals throughout the caption.

## Editing Checks

- **Exact label parity:** Every condition name, axis title, and legend term in the graphic must match the caption and main text verbatim.
- **Self-contained for skimmers:** A reader scanning only the figures and captions should grasp the paper's story without hunting backward for symbol definitions.
- **No numerical drift:** Every headline number or bound quoted in a caption or subtitle must match the locked data file plotted in that figure.
